The Diamond Hands Crypto Tracker - Android Mobile App

Project Overview

  • An Honours Degree project turned passion project, focused on Android mobile app development.
  • Aimed at new-and-existing cryptocurrency investors as the all-in-one solution for price tracking and market news.
  • Built using Flutter for the front-end UI, Dart for the app's business logic, and Firebase's Firestore for backend data processing operations and authentication.
  • Keep tabs on development progress via the project's repository.
